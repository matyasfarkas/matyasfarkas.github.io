    FSAP-expansion feasibility — data-availability audit (6 candidate blocs + 4 cross-cutting workstreams)¶

    Purpose. Move the tool toward full FSAP surveillance coverage — the IMF's Financial Sector Assessment Program roster of 32 mandatory-every-5-years + 15 mandatory-every-10-years = 47 jurisdictions. The deployed model carries 50 economies today, of which 41 of the 47 FSAP jurisdictions are already covered. This audit sources the remaining named gaps and the cross-cutting data a global-solve FSAP tool needs (time-varying trade weights, complete NEER, complete exports/imports, and an external-balance / NFA satellite for every bloc). Requested and sourced 2026-07-17. Ingredients verified by live API fetch; the no-synthetic-data rule applies throughout — annual-only real GDP is not interpolated to quarterly, ragged edges are kept, nothing is back-filled.

    Everything in this document is STAGED, not integrated. All artifacts live under pipeline/fsap_expansion_staging/ and no panel (DataRaw_<CC>_glob.json), spec, app/, app/public/, or .m/.ts/.py production file has been touched. This is a sourced-and-staged plan: the series exist on disk and are validated, but no re-estimation into the model has run. Building any of it is gated on the parked decisions in §8 and a single combined re-estimation.

    1. FSAP coverage goal and the six gap targets¶
    FSAP roster 32 five-year + 15 ten-year = 47 jurisdictions
    Currently in the tool 50 economies deployed; 41 of the 47 FSAP jurisdictions covered
    Named gap targets sourced this session HK, SG, PE, PH, RO (candidate new blocs) + AE
    Verdict 5 of 6 VIABLE (HK SG RO PE PH); AE NOT VIABLE (annual-only real GDP)

    Sourcing 5 of the 6 (all but AE) would take FSAP-jurisdiction coverage from 41 toward 46 of 47. AE cannot be built to the tool's standard from the approved APIs (see §2, AE) and is deferred, not faked.

    2. New candidate blocs — per-bloc verdict¶

    Real-GDP is the binding ingredient: a bloc is VIABLE only with a genuine quarterly real-GDP series (the panel's GDP_<CC> anchor). Verified spans below are read from the staged pipeline/fsap_expansion_staging/newblocs/<CC>/GDP_<CC>.json (source/dates/values), with the core-block companions in the same folder and X/M in pipeline/fsap_expansion_staging/trade/.

    HK FULL VIABLE HK C&SD Table 310-31007 (SA, chained-2024 HK$m) 1990Q1–2026Q1 (145) none material 10Y yield short (2010Q1–2024Q4)
    SG FULL VIABLE SingStat Table M015662 (SA, chained-2015 S$m) 1975Q1–2026Q1 (205) no policy rate / no 10Y ER-regime economy — NEER is the instrument
    RO FULL Eurostat namq_10_gdp Q.CLV20_MNAC.SCA.B1GQ.RO 1995Q1–2026Q1 (125) none Eurostat drop-in, exactly like PL/HU/CZ
    PE FULL BCRP series PN02538AQ (real, S/ 2007, NSA) 1990Q1–2026Q1 (145) none NSA expenditure block → seasonal-dummy candidate

    HK — Hong Kong SAR (FULL VIABLE)¶

    Genuine quarterly real GDP from the Census & Statistics Department (Table 310-31007, seasonally adjusted, chained-2024 dollars), 1990Q1–2026Q1 (145 q) — newblocs/HK/GDP_HK.json. Companions staged: CPI, equity (EQ), house prices (HP), a 3-month HIBOR policy proxy (RATE_HK, HKMA, 1996Q3–2026Q2), a 10-year benchmark yield (YIELD10_HK, HKMA, 2010Q1–2024Q4, 60 q — short and ending 2024Q4, flag), and BIS credit-to-private-sector (CREDIT_HK, 1990Q4–2025Q4). Exports/imports are staged in trade/EXP_HK.json / trade/IMP_HK.json (C&SD Table 310-31003, chained volumes, 1973Q1–2026Q1, 213 q). Verdict: FULL VIABLE — a complete real+financial core; the only caveat is the shorter, 2024Q4-ending 10Y yield tail.

    SG — Singapore (FULL VIABLE)¶

    Genuine quarterly real GDP from SingStat (Table M015662, SA, chained-2015 dollars), 1975Q1–2026Q1 (205 q) — the longest history of the six (newblocs/SG/GDP_SG.json). Companions: CPI, equity, house prices, BIS credit level + credit/GDP (CREDIT_SG, CREDIT_GDP_SG). Exports/imports staged in trade/EXP_SG.json / trade/IMP_SG.json (SingStat M014812, chained volumes, 1975Q1–2026Q1, 205 q). Structural note: Singapore runs an exchange-rate-centred monetary regime — there is no conventional policy rate or 10Y benchmark to source, and none is staged (RATE_SG/YIELD10_SG intentionally absent). The exchange rate is the policy instrument, so the staged NEER_SG carries the monetary channel. Verdict: FULL VIABLE, with the FX-regime treatment declared.

    RO — Romania (FULL)¶

    Eurostat drop-in, identical machinery to the existing PL/HU/CZ blocs. Real GDP from namq_10_gdp key Q.CLV20_MNAC.SCA.B1GQ.RO (chain-linked vol. ref-2020, RON, seasonally + calendar adjusted), 1995Q1–2026Q1 (125 q) — newblocs/RO/GDP_RO.json, built by newblocs/RO/build_RO.py (Eurostat SDMX + BIS). Full companion set: HICP (CPI_RO), 10Y Maastricht yield (YIELD10_RO), gross debt/GDP (DEBT_GDP_RO), consumption/investment/government (CONS/INV/GOV), house prices, policy rate, and EXP/IMP. Verdict: FULL — no new provider, no seasonal seam (SCA), slots straight into the Eurostat pipeline.

    PE — Peru (FULL)¶

    Real national accounts from BCRP (series PN02538AQ, PBI por tipo de gasto, millones S/ 2007), 1990Q1–2026Q1 (145 q) — newblocs/PE/GDP_PE.json, built by newblocs/PE/build_PE.py (BCRP API + BIS). Full companion set including 10Y yield (YIELD10_PE), credit (CREDIT_PE), equity, CPI (IPC), consumption/investment/government, house prices, policy rate, and EXP/IMP. Seasonality caveat (per the build docstring): the BCRP expenditure block is the raw, non-seasonally-adjusted national-accounts series — BCRP publishes a desestacionalizado PBI total but no SA expenditure decomposition — so PE's real block is a seasonal-dummy candidate under the tool's seasonal machinery. Verdict: FULL, with the NSA flag.

    PH — Philippines (FULL core; needs a financial tail)¶

    Genuine quarterly SA real GDP from the PSA OpenSTAT SANA table 012B5BEXS1 (constant-2018 prices, seasonally adjusted), 2000Q1–2026Q1 (105 q) — newblocs/PH/GDP_PH.json, built by newblocs/PH/build_PH.py (PSA PxWeb + BIS). Staged companions: CPI (three era-partitioned PSA sub-tables on one 2018=100 base — a concatenation of identical-base official series, not fabrication), consumption/investment/government, house prices, policy rate, and EXP/IMP. Missing for a full financial block: a 10Y benchmark yield and a credit series — neither YIELD10_PH nor CREDIT_PH is staged. Verdict: FULL core (real + price + FX + rate), but a yield + credit tail must be sourced before PH matches the financial depth of the other blocs.

    AE — United Arab Emirates (NOT VIABLE)¶

    The UAE has no genuine quarterly real GDP. Only annual constant-price GDP exists (World Bank WDI NY.GDP.MKTP.KN, AED, 1970–2024) — staged for provenance only as newblocs/AE/GDP_AE_ANNUAL_reference.json, explicitly not a panel input. Quarterly UAE GDP is published only at current prices (nominal), which cannot serve as a real-activity anchor, and the no-synthetic-data rule forbids interpolating the annual real series to quarterly. NEER_AE was still sourced (BIS, in neer/) but is moot without a real anchor. Verdict: NOT VIABLE as a bloc from the approved APIs; revisit only if a national-source quarterly real GDP (e.g. UAE Federal Competitiveness & Statistics Centre) is authorised.

    3. Time-varying trade weights + the honest CN/EA trajectory finding¶

    The GVAR couples blocs through a bilateral trade-weight matrix. Today the solve uses a single static weights_full.json; FSAP surveillance wants the weights to reflect the decade of the shock, not one frozen year. weights/build_weights_timevarying.py reconstructs the full per-year weight panel from IMF IMTS, following the tool's own recipe verbatim (endpoint api.imf.org/external/sdmx/2.1/data/IMF.STA,IMTS,1.0.0/, key order COUNTRY.INDICATOR.COUNTERPART_COUNTRY.A, indicators XG_FOB_USD + MG_CIF_USD, euro area = G163 aggregate, same <0.05%-share drop + renormalize + 6-dp residual-adjust rule as the static file).

    • Scope: the 33 currently-coupled blocs + the 6 FSAP candidates = 39 reporters, annual 1995–2024 (weights/build_weights_timevarying.py, BLOC_CODES).
    • Outputs (staged): weights/weights_timevarying.json (every year 1995–2024), weights/weights_latest.json (2024, the solve-time default candidate), weights/weights_validation.json. Raw SDMX cached per reporter in weights/raw/imts_<CC>.xml.
    • Integrity: all 30 years × 39 rows sum to 1 within 1e-6 (row_sums_all_pass_1e-6: true; max deviation after residual-adjust 1.1e-16); no omitted reporter-years (omitted_rows: {}); RU/TW partner-side mirror fallback only, no fabrication or carry-forward.

    The honest CN/EA trajectory finding. The build's a-priori monotonicity hypotheses (US→CN rising, US→EA falling, EA→CN rising over 1995–2024) all returned false — and on inspection these are genuine data features, not bugs (weights_validation.json → trajectory_findings):

    US-row weight 1995 trajectory 2024
    → CN 4.75% rises to a 17.6% peak in 2017, then falls after the 2018 trade war 11.9%
    → EA 15.2% roughly flat ~15% (2012 min 14.2%), then RISES — the data does not show a falling EA share 17.8%
    EA → CN 3.4% rises to a 14.0% peak in 2020/21, then eases 12.9%

    The key correction: the post-2018 decline in the US import basket is all China, not Europe — the US→EA share actually rose to 17.8% by 2024. A static-2024 weight matrix would bake in the post-decoupling China share; a trailing-average or as-of-shock-year matrix would look very different mid-2000s. This is exactly why the year of the solve-time weights is a parked decision (§8).

    4. NEER coverage completion¶

    neer/build_neer.py pulls BIS broad nominal effective exchange rates (WS_EER, key M.N.B.<CC>), quarterly-averages the monthly index over complete quarters only (all 3 months observed — no partial-quarter averages), and writes one neer/NEER_<CC>.json per bloc. Genuine data only: where a live panel exists the series is truncated to the panel's own quarterly grid span, never extended or padded.

    • Gaps filled (existing blocs that lacked a nominal EER): US, JP, KR, TR, TW, and CN (the CN panel carried only REER_CN — a nominal EER is added). Each truncated to its panel span.
    • Candidates: HK, SG, PE, PH, RO, AE — no panel yet, so the full genuine BIS span is kept.
    • Staged: neer/NEER_{US,JP,KR,TR,TW,CN,HK,SG,PE,PH,RO,AE}.json (12 files). Example: NEER_JP.json = BIS broad nominal, 2000Q1–2025Q4 (104 q). This closes NEER coverage for every gap bloc and every viable candidate; NEER also feeds the NFA valuation term (§6).

    5. Exports / imports coverage (the RU-IMP gap)¶

    Audited in trade/COVERAGE_AUDIT.md. X/M is effectively complete for every viable bloc:

    • 49 of 50 coupled blocs ALREADY carry real EXP_<CC>/IMP_<CC> (or XH/MH for the US) in the shipped panels — no sourcing needed.
    • The one gap: RU has EXP but no IMP (a sanctions-era ragged edge; the genuine RU import mirror trade/IMP_RU.json sourced here — FRED/OECD QNA NAEXKP07RUQ661S — ends 2021Q3, which is itself the evidence behind the RU keep/drop question in §8).
    • Candidates: EXP/IMP staged for HK, SG, PE, PH, RO (trade/EXP_*.json, trade/IMP_*.json; PE/PH/RO also carry their own copies inside newblocs/<CC>/). AE not staged — moot, since AE is not a viable bloc.

    Net: X/M coverage is complete for all five viable candidates and all existing blocs; the only open item is RU IMP, tied to the RU keep/drop decision.

    6. NFA / external-balance extension¶

    Goal: extend the shipped balance-of-payments satellite from the 3 live blocs (US, EA, CN) to the full 50-bloc GVAR + the FSAP candidates, mirroring the fiscal block's division of labour (the BVAR forecasts the flow; the stock is an accounting recursion). Design + sourcing in nfa/DESIGN.md.

    6.1 What the shipped recursion consumes (ground truth)¶

    computeExternalBlock() in app/src/lib/compute/externalAccounting.ts is a side-effect-free per-quarter recursion that reads 5 estimated LEVEL-space drivers — exports, imports, gdp, deflator, fx (positional idx into the forecast path) — and 3 anchor constants from external_meta.json: nfa0 (sourced net-IIP/GDP seed), iNFA (imputed NFA return, modeling default 3 %/yr), fxShare (FX-exposed share, default 0.4). The identity, per quarter, seeded b_{-1}=nfa0:

    tb_t  = 100·(X_t − M_t)/Y_t
ca_t  = tb_t + iNFA·b_{t-1}/100            # income leg IMPUTED
val_t = −fxShare·b_{t-1}·Δℓ^fx_t/100       # FX revaluation
b_t   = b_{t-1}/(1 + gⁿ_t) + ca_t/4 + val_t

    So today the trade balance is derived from estimated real trade volumes and the income leg is imputed, not sourced. That carries two costs: (i) tb is a real-volume/real-GDP ratio, not the nominal customs CA; (ii) the units landmine — X, M, Y must share one nominal scale, which holds for US/EA but fails for essentially every EM bloc (it forced CN's bespoke units.* reconciliation). JP, UK, CA were held out only because their IIP was "not yet verified."

    6.2 IMF-IIP unblocks JP/UK/CA (and 48 more)¶

    The IIP blocker is now removed. IMF IIP delivers a quarterly net-IIP anchor (SDMX IMF.STA,IIP/{ISO3}.NETAL_P.NIIP.USD.Q) for JP/UK/CA and dozens of others, and IMF BOP delivers a quarterly current account (IMF.STA,BOP/{ISO3}.NETCD_T.CAB.USD.Q). Sourced this task (nfa/build_nfa_external.py, IMF + World Bank, no key):

    • nfa/CA_<CC>.json (53 blocs) — quarterly current account, full-history USD level (values_usd, genuine IMF, ragged edge kept) plus CA as %GDP annualised (values = 400·CABq / WB annual GDP NY.GDP.MKTP.CD; null where a year has no WB GDP — no interpolation).
    • nfa/nfa_anchors.json (54 blocs) — the nfa0 seed, 100·netIIP(USD)/annual nominal GDP(USD); US/EA/CN keep their shipped-live values with the IMF/WB recompute recorded as a cross-check.

    Sign/magnitude validation (all genuine): US −66.75, JP +79 (largest creditor), CH +117, NO +377 (SWF), SG +181, HK +587 (financial entrepôt), GR −136 / IE −60 / PT −52 / ES −47 (periphery), CN +20.4 (matches shipped +20.35). CA flows: US −2.5…−3.9%, GR −11%, RU +2.3%, MY +1.6% — all correct-signed.

    6.3 Feasibility split (nfa/DESIGN.md §5)¶

    • FULL — 54 blocs (quarterly IMF CA + IMF/ECB net-IIP anchor): the 3 live (US/EA/CN) + 51 new, incl. JP/UK/CA (the previously-held-out three) and FSAP candidates HK PE PH RO SG. Ragged edges respected under the GVAR uniform-cutoff rule (e.g. IE CA ends 2025Q1, MY 2024Q4).
    • PARTIAL — 1: AE — IMF BOP has annual CA only, no quarterly CA and no IIP; ships without an external block (like JP/UK/CA did), no annual→quarterly back-fill.
    • NOT-FEASIBLE — 1: TW — absent from IMF BOP/IIP entirely (and WB GDP); a national CBC/DGBAS pull is outside the approved APIs; ships without an external block, no fabrication.

    6.4 Design recommendation — Option B (estimate the CA share)¶

    Two ways to wire NFA into the model (nfa/DESIGN.md §3):

    • Option A (status quo): keep tb = 100·(X−M)/Y + imputed income. Requires a CN-style per-bloc units reconciliation for every non-common-scale (EM) bloc; the TB stays a real-volume proxy and iNFA stays imputed.
    • Option B (RECOMMENDED, fiscal-mirror): add an estimated panel column CA_GDP_<CC> (current account, %GDP, quarterly) exactly as FREV_GDP/FPEXP_GDP are estimated in the fiscal block, then roll only the stock: b_t = b_{t-1}/(1+gⁿ_t) + ca_t/4 + val_t, seeded at nfa0. Why B: (1) it mirrors the requested fiscal division of labour (estimate the flow share, derive the stock); (2) it uses the genuine nominal BoP current account (income + transfers in the data) so iNFA is retired — no imputed return, no double-count; (3) CA/GDP is unit-free, so the CN units reconciliation is unnecessary and one uniform recipe covers all 54 feasible blocs; (4) the trade agent's X/M still feed the BVAR as drivers but are no longer load-bearing for NFA. fxShare = 0.4 remains a flagged default for the valuation leg only.

    Building Option B needs (app-team, out of scope here): a new CA_GDP_<CC> panel column per feasible bloc, a one-branch change in externalAccounting.ts to accept an estimated ca path (skip the volume-TB + imputed-income branch when a caIdx is present), and a re-estimation. NFA adds no cross-bloc coupling (passive per-bloc recursion) → no coupling-cache regen, orthogonal to the GVAR solve, exactly like the fiscal block.

    8. Parked decisions (user)¶

    1. RU keep / drop. RU is FSAP-listed, but genuine RU data ends ~2021 (the sourced RU import mirror trade/IMP_RU.json ends 2021Q3; existing RU series already carry tail-possibly-WEO-synthetic flags). Keep for FSAP completeness with a documented ragged edge, or drop for data integrity? (Ties to the RU-IMP gap in §5 and the fiscal audit's RU=NOT-FEASIBLE.)
    2. Solve-time weights year. Use 2024 (weights/weights_latest.json, bakes in the post-decoupling US→CN share) or a trailing average / as-of-shock-year slice from weights/weights_timevarying.json? The honest CN/EA trajectory finding (§3) makes this consequential.
    3. NFA Option A vs B. Recommend B (estimate CA_GDP_<CC>, retire iNFA, unit-free, one uniform recipe) — but it adds a panel column and a re-estimation; A is the fallback for any bloc where an estimated CA share is undesirable.
    4. World Bank GDP-USD denominator sign-off. The NFA anchors and CA/GDP shares use WB annual nominal USD GDP (NY.GDP.MKTP.CD) as the denominator against a quarter-end IIP stock (a ≤1Q vintage seam, negligible for a seed; e.g. US recompute −69.1 vs shipped −66.75 is purely FRED-SAAR-2026Q1 vs WB-annual-2025). Sign off on WB as the denominator source, or switch to a trailing-4-quarter nominal-USD GDP for an exact vintage match?
    5. AE / TW external treatment. AE (annual CA only, no IIP) and TW (absent from IMF BOP/IIP entirely) ship without an external block and without back-fill — confirm, or authorise a national-source pull (UAE FCSC; TW CBC/DGBAS) outside the approved APIs? AE's bloc itself is also NOT VIABLE (§2) pending a national quarterly real-GDP source.
